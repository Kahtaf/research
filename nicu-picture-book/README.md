# NICU / New-Sibling Picture Books

Two picture-book manuscripts for ages ~2–5, developed and refined in this folder, plus a full art-direction bible for the finished one.

## The books

### 1. When You Get Here  ·  *the finished book*
An older brother speaks to his baby sister who "isn't here yet," listing all the wonders he'll show her once she's home. Deliberately **metaphor-light**: the NICU is never named — it lives in the subtext ("somewhere warm and far away," "getting stronger," the long wait) — so it also reads as a sweet, universal book for any family expecting a baby. The refrain and title is *"When you get here,"* and the book flips from future tense to present tense at the homecoming.

- **`when-you-get-here-vA-brothers-welcome.md`** — the **LOCKED** final script (12 spreads, ~285 words). Reviewed twice by fresh editor + NICU-parent passes; both called it ready.
- **`when-you-get-here-art-direction.md`** — production-ready art-direction bible for AI image generation (Nano Banana / OpenAI ImageGen): global style token, character bible (the boy, the two cats, Mama, Papa, the baby), setting bible, lighting arc, 12 paste-ready spread prompts, a cover prompt, and a consistency checklist.

**Packaging note:** the book promises a homecoming and delivers it — joyful for expecting families, but a dedication / jacket line should quietly signal it's a book for families *awaiting* a homecoming.

### 2. The Smallest Light  ·  *a separate, earlier book*
A quiet, lyrical bedtime NICU story ("a small light for a small girl" → "one big light for one small girl"), refined against the craft of classic children's books.

- **`the-smallest-light-refined.md`** — refined manuscript with per-spread rationale.
